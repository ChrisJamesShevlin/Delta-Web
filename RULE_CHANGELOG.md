# Rule changelog

Every change to a trading or staking rule goes here with its date, so results can be split into before and after (`python3 -m ig_swing.challenge review` reads the newest date). A change is judged on ideas written AFTER it, never by re-scoring old ones. One change at a time, and say what you expect it to do before looking at the numbers. Entries start with `- YYYY-MM-DD`.

- 2026-11-11 Challenge profile started: ceiling 10% of equity at risk per trade, 5% until my stated probabilities are shown to be honest (30 or more finished ideas within 10 points of the hit rate), 1/8 Kelly, floor at £15, cooldown of 24 hours after two losing trades, drawdown brake at 25% below the peak, at most two new trades per UTC day.
- 2026-11-11 Position management started: cut losers early when the thesis is broken, tighten stops (never widen), trailing stop from +1R at 1R behind the best price, take-profit extended while the move is working.
- 2026-11-11 Mechanical baseline started: long if the price is above its 20-day average, else short; 1.5 ATR stop, 3 ATR target, 5 days; one per market per UTC day from the first briefing of the day; shares excluded.
- 2026-11-11 Shadow book scores each idea four ways: fixed stop and target, and a trailing stop from +1R at 1R, 1.5R and 2R behind the best price.
- 2026-11-12 Staking changed (user go): until my stated probabilities are calibrated (30 or more finished ideas within 10 points of the hit rate), every live trade is staked at the market's minimum stake. Kelly stays as a skip filter (zero or negative Kelly means no trade) and its figures are still printed. Once calibrated, the stake follows 1/8 Kelly again, capped at 10%. Expected effect: stakes no larger than before and often smaller, so fewer ruinous outcomes if my probabilities are too high; results are judged only on ideas written from this date.
- 2026-11-12 Selection changed (user instruction: the account exists to gather data, so being too cautious defeats it): when every code guardrail passes (order allowed, Kelly positive on an honestly stated chance, spread normal) I take one live trade per check even at LOW confidence, and say so. Margin share is no longer a refusal in the briefing: a market is shadow-only only if the order cannot be placed (the 60% margin cap was a briefing label the order code never enforced); heavy margin is shown as a warning. No limit in the risk engine changed. Expected effect: more live trades, mostly low-confidence, analysed separately by confidence level; results are judged on trades from this date.
- 2026-11-12 (later, corrects the entry above, user clarification) Selection: the account is for data, but a trade needs a genuine, confident directional view, never a guess or a coin flip. When I have that view and every guardrail passes, I take the trade without holding back out of excess caution; with no view, no trade (shadow only). The NVIDIA sell opened earlier the same day (idea 4, p 0.38 stated) was a guess with an inflated win chance and does not meet this rule. The margin-label fix stands.
- 2026-11-12 Price setups added as a fixed playbook (user: if we only check the news we will never trade). Criteria, written before any result: (1) BREAKOUT/BREAKDOWN, playbook `trend`: the briefing's 20-day range position is above 100% (go long) or below 0% (go short) and IG clients are not already 70% or more positioned in the direction of the trade; (2) FADE, playbook `other`: a move of more than 2 ATRs with no cause I can find, traded against the move. Each trigger is recorded as an idea with its own honest win chance (about 0.36 for a 2 to 1 reward, a little above a coin flip); at most one live trade per check. DISCLOSURE: a 0.5 ATR same-day move filter was proposed in chat and dropped the same hour because it would have excluded AUD/USD, the setup that prompted the rule; so the first ideas under this rule (2026-11-12 AUD/USD, France 40, Germany 40) were chosen with the criteria and the data in view and are not clean evidence for it. Judged only on ideas from this date, by playbook, against the baseline; no tuning before about 30 results.
- 2026-11-12 (later) Daily cap on new trades removed (user instruction: trade as and when, the cap was arbitrary). `max_new_trades_per_day` is 0 (no limit) for the challenge profile; it was 2. - 2026-11-13 Risk ceilings raised (user instruction: willing to lose the whole £50 over the test, not on one single trade). Per-trade risk ceiling 20% of equity (was 10%) and total open risk 40% (was 20%), so two full-size trades can be open together. Unchanged: two open positions, the £15 floor, the cooldown after two losing trades, the drawdown brake, loss limits (25% daily, 40% weekly), the mandatory stop, the minimum stake until calibrated (Kelly is a skip filter, its 5% then 10% staking cap untouched). - 2026-11-13 (later) Basket awareness added (user request), recording only, nothing blocks an order. Every idea now stores its theme exposure, an overlap warning against open positions, and a confirmation label from the latest briefing: the share of correlated markets (same theme, same direction, with a 20-day average) whose price is on the same side of it. Labels fixed now: confirmed (60% or more agree, at least 2 markets), contradicted (40% or fewer), mixed, too_few. `place` prints overlap warnings. `challenge review` splits results by confirmation label. My working rule: a new idea that shares a theme with an open position is treated as the same bet and normally skipped. Expected effect: none on results until about 30 finished ideas per label; judged on ideas from this date, and confirmation is a hypothesis, not a filter, until the numbers say so.
- 2026-11-13 (later still) Open position count cap removed (user instruction: we can place whatever we want while gathering data). `max_open_positions` is 50 for the challenge profile, effectively no limit; it was 2. The real limits are now total open risk 40% of equity, margin, the loss limits, the £15 floor, the cooldown, the drawdown brake and the mandatory stop. Unchanged: the working rule that a new idea sharing a theme with an open position is the same bet and normally skipped. Expected effect: more simultaneous positions when there are several confident, unrelated views; the 40% open-risk limit will usually bind first. Judged on trades from this date.
- 2026-11-13 (last) Same-theme working rule removed (user instruction: trade whatever looks like we should). Before, a new idea sharing a theme with an open position was normally skipped live and recorded as shadow. Now it is allowed when I have my own confident reason and every guardrail passes. Kept: overlap and confirmation are still recorded on every idea and `review` splits by them; I tell the user when a trade shares a theme and how much risk sits on it; total open risk 40% still applies. Opposing views still close the first position and enter the new one. Expected effect: more simultaneous correlated positions, so one move can stop several out together; stakes are the minimum so the money is small. Judged on trades from this date; revisit before any stake increase after calibration.
- 2026-11-16 Shadow scoring extended, recording only, nothing live changes (user question: is +1R too late to start the trailing stop?). Live rule unchanged: the trail still starts at +1R. Five trail variants are now tried in all (three widths, two earlier starts), so a small gap between them is noise; no change to the live trigger on fewer than about 30 finished ideas. Applies to ideas scored from now on; older ideas are rescored with the new variants too because scoring uses stored entry, stop and bars only.
- 2026-11-16 (data) Price-bar sourcing changed after IG's weekly price allowance (10,000 points, resets Wednesday) ran down to 45. Routine pulls keep a 300-point reserve; reads of a market being traded may use it. When the allowance is short the briefing, `quick` and `idea` fall back to Yahoo bars rescaled to IG's price and say so; those ideas carry `bar_source: yahoo_scaled`. Measured on 20 days of IG against Yahoo: index ATRs about 20% below IG's, silver 37% below, natural gas 37% above, FX day boundaries differ, range position off by 5 points on average (18 worst). Working rule: a Yahoo-based read is context only, never the basis for a stop size or a breakout/breakdown call, so no live price-setup order is placed on it; shadow ideas may be recorded and are flagged. Scoring (`report`, `review`, `place` calibration) skips an idea it cannot price from IG and says so, never scoring on other data. No trading rule, stake or limit changed.

- 2026-11-19 (research, no rule change) Pre-registered stop-rule test run once (`ig_swing/stop_rule_test.py`, result in CLAUDE.md): over 2,866 trend-playbook trades on Yahoo daily bars 2016 to 2026, stop rules differ by about 0.01R a trade or less (inside the noise), breakeven-only rules leave 24 to 31% of good trades ending at zero or worse, and the 19 Nov rule cuts that to 5%. The 19 Nov rule stays. The trend entries themselves show no edge (-0.02R, SE 0.03).
