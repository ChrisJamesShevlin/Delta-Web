# Rule changelog

Every change to a trading or staking rule goes here with its date, so results can be split into before and after (`python3 -m ig_swing.challenge review` reads the newest date). A change is judged on ideas written AFTER it, never by re-scoring old ones. One change at a time, and say what you expect it to do before looking at the numbers. Entries start with `- YYYY-MM-DD`.

- 2026-11-11 Challenge profile started: ceiling 10% of equity at risk per trade, 5% until my stated probabilities are shown to be honest (30 or more finished ideas within 10 points of the hit rate), 1/8 Kelly, floor at £15, cooldown of 24 hours after two losing trades, drawdown brake at 25% below the peak, at most two new trades per UTC day.
- 2026-11-11 Position management started: cut losers early when the thesis is broken, tighten stops (never widen), trailing stop from +1R at 1R behind the best price, take-profit extended while the move is working.
- 2026-11-11 Mechanical baseline started: long if the price is above its 20-day average, else short; 1.5 ATR stop, 3 ATR target, 5 days; one per market per UTC day from the first briefing of the day; shares excluded.
- 2026-11-11 Shadow book scores each idea four ways: fixed stop and target, and a trailing stop from +1R at 1R, 1.5R and 2R behind the best price.
- 2026-11-12 Staking changed (user go): until my stated probabilities are calibrated (30 or more finished ideas within 10 points of the hit rate), every live trade is staked at the market's minimum stake. Kelly stays as a skip filter (zero or negative Kelly means no trade) and its figures are still printed. Once calibrated, the stake follows 1/8 Kelly again, capped at 10%. Expected effect: stakes no larger than before and often smaller, so fewer ruinous outcomes if my probabilities are too high; results are judged only on ideas written from this date.
- 2026-11-12 Selection changed (user instruction: the account exists to gather data, so being too cautious defeats it): when every code guardrail passes (order allowed, Kelly positive on an honestly stated chance, spread normal) I take one live trade per check even at LOW confidence, and say so. Margin share is no longer a refusal in the briefing: a market is shadow-only only if the order cannot be placed (the 60% margin cap was a briefing label the order code never enforced); heavy margin is shown as a warning. No limit in the risk engine changed. Expected effect: more live trades, mostly low-confidence, analysed separately by confidence level; results are judged on trades from this date.
- 2026-11-12 (later, corrects the entry above, user clarification) Selection: the account is for data, but a trade needs a genuine, confident directional view, never a guess or a coin flip. When I have that view and every guardrail passes, I take the trade without holding back out of excess caution; with no view, no trade (shadow only).
